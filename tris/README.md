# TRIS submission materials — Versemend

This folder holds the TDS TRIS (Technology Request Information Sheet) submission materials for **Versemend** (version 1.0.0), prepared 2026-11-11. Versemend was submitted together with ONTrack, LightShow, Fathom, the Salesforce MCP integration, and Niflheim in one combined email to DLT-IT TRIS Request; the combined package (the email text, the full six-product worksheet, and the source form/process documents) is kept in the requestor's submission records and is not published here.

## Contents

- [WORKSHEET.md](WORKSHEET.md) — every requestor-facing TRIS form field for Versemend, pre-filled from the submission evidence; open items are marked **[INPUT NEEDED]**.
- [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md) — one line per TRIS requirement: where the answer is entered (form section and row, and the process step at which it is submitted) and its status, with links to the satisfying evidence in this folder.
- [BUSINESS-USE.md](BUSINESS-USE.md) — the business case at TDS: self-paced, verifiable Lua training for technical staff.
